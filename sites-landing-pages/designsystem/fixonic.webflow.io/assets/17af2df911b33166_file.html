Help center

Got a Question?

Quick answers to questions you may have. Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch with us.
How long does the free diagnostic take?
The complete vehicle inspection takes approximately 30 to 45 minutes. You will receive a detailed report immediately after the assessment.
Do I need to book a slot in advance for the $0 audit?
Booking in advance is recommended to secure your preferred time, although walk-in appointments may be available depending on our daily schedule.
What is the difference between Ceramic Coating and PPF?
Ceramic coating enhances gloss and chemical resistance, while PPF provides stronger physical protection against stone chips, scratches, and road debris.
How many stages of paint correction do you perform?
We offer one-, two-, and multi-stage paint correction depending on the condition of your paintwork and the finish you want to achieve.
Is there any hidden obligation with the free service?
No. Our complimentary inspection comes with no hidden fees or commitments. You decide whether to proceed after reviewing our recommendations.
Have specific vehicle questions? Ask us during your free diagnostic!
