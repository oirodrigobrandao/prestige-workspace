About Us

Discover our passion for automotive excellence, where advanced detailing, precision craftsmanship, and dedicated service redefine vehicle care.

THE STANDARD FOR ELITE AUTOMOTIVE CARE

Fixonic is an elite automotive detailing studio dedicated to protecting the appearance and performance of every vehicle. We combine expert craftsmanship with advanced surface treatments to deliver lasting protection and exceptional results.

From precision paint correction to premium ceramic coating, each service is completed with meticulous attention to detail. Our goal is to preserve every vehicle’s character while ensuring a flawless, showroom-quality finish.
Our Automotive Philosophy
We believe exceptional automotive care begins with precision, premium materials, and an uncompromising commitment to quality.
The Vision Behind Fixonic
We specialize in a wide range of automotive services, including detailing, ceramic coating, paint correction, and vehicle protection. Our expertise covers both everyday and luxury cars, always focusing on quality, precision, and reliable results.

How It Works

Simple Process

From your first message to a spotless handoff, here's exactly what to expect when you book with us.
Step 1 · Same-day reply
Request Your Quote
Text (603) 825-3880 or fill out the online form. We reply the same day with transparent package pricing — no phone tag, no pushy upsells.
Step 2 · You pick the day
Book & Schedule
Pick a drop-off time that works for you and bring the vehicle to our Derry, NH shop. We'll confirm the appointment and walk you through what to expect when you arrive.
Step 3 · 2–8 hrs in-shop
The Detail Itself
Depending on the package, we spend two to eight hours hand-washing, steam-cleaning, and paint-correcting. Every vehicle is treated like our own, panel by panel, seat by seat.
Step 4 · Satisfaction guarantee
Walkaround & Reveal
We walk the finish with you before we pack up. If anything isn't showroom-ready, we fix it on the spot — every service is backed by our 100% satisfaction guarantee.
